1 · Morning mobility

Two rounds. Smooth and easy, wake the body up.

  1. Dynamic hip flexor stretch each side
  2. Dynamic frog stretch
  3. Plate tea cup 6 each side
  4. Dynamic pigeon stretch each side
  5. Wide leg seated forward fold
  6. Superband dislocates 6
  7. T-spine rotation with reach each side
  8. Side-lying IT band stretch each side
  9. World's greatest stretch 5 each side
  10. Seated hip twist 5 each side

2 · Off ice warm-up

One round, in order, ~10 m of hallway. Build the pace, last three fast.

  1. Shuttle run 10 m
  2. High knee skipping 10 m
  3. Runner stretch 4 each side
  4. Inchworm walk 5
  5. Down dog toe tap to push-up 4 each side
  6. 90-90 hip switch to lift & reach 4 each side
  7. Push-up to T-rotation 3 each side
  8. Pogo hops 10
  9. Single leg lateral pogo hops 5 each
  10. Glute bridge 5, 2 s hold
  11. Double broad jump 3
  12. Backpedal to sprint 2 × 5 m back, 10 m forward

3 · Post skate cool-down

Two rounds. Keep every stretch gentle.

  1. Cat-cow 4
  2. Thread the needle 3 each side
  3. Dynamic hip flexor stretch each side
  4. Dynamic pigeon stretch each side
  5. Rear foot elevated hip flexor stretch each side
  6. Side-lying IT band stretch each side
  7. Dynamic frog stretch
  8. Straddle side stretch 3 each side, easy

Fuel between skates

Decided by one thing: how long until the next ice. Fats take a back seat until the evening.

Under 2 hrs: a little easy digesting protein + a light carb, fats low. Whey or protein powder in water, Fairlife, skim milk. A banana or a few dates.

2 to 4 hrs: carbs first + some protein. Pears, bananas, oranges, dates, raisins, apricots, toast with honey or jam, plus Fairlife, yogurt, or a shake. Then the 2 hr snack as it gets close.

4 hrs +: a full meal, fats low. Chicken or turkey, pasta / rice / quinoa, vegetables. Then the 2 hr snack.

The whole week in one line: sleep 9.5 plus, 3 litres of water, screens off an hour before bed, nothing new, warm before the ice, cool down after, fuel by the gap, and eat big once the skating is done.
