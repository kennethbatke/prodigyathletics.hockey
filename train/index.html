Is 9 to 12 too young for strength training?

Not the way I do it. At this age it's movement quality, bodyweight strength, coordination, and the habits that make everything later work. No heavy loading, nothing they'll outgrow.

What happens on the one on one call?

Once the plan is built, you and your player join me for a call. I walk through the program, the schedule, and the nutrition plan, answer questions, and adjust anything that doesn't fit your real week. The goal is a plan they'll actually follow.

What's the difference between the two options?

Same build, same call. With Coached, I stay involved after: I review their form in the app, give feedback, adjust the plan as they develop, and answer questions as they come up. With Self-Guided, you take the plan and run it yourselves.
